Admission Requirements

General Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Official high school transcripts or equivalent
  • Standardized test scores (SAT or ACT)
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • Personal statement or essay
  • Application fee ($50 for domestic students, $75 for international students)

International Students

  • All general requirements
  • English proficiency test scores (TOEFL, IELTS, or equivalent)
  • Financial documentation showing ability to cover tuition and living expenses
  • Copy of passport
  • Credential evaluation for non-U.S. academic documents

Transfer Students

  • All general requirements
  • Official college transcripts from all previously attended institutions
  • Good academic standing at previous institution
  • Minimum GPA of 2.5 on a 4.0 scale
  • Course descriptions for transfer credit evaluation
