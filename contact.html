Frequently Asked Questions

How can I schedule a campus tour?

You can schedule a campus tour by contacting our Admissions Office at (123) 456-7891 or by filling out the campus visit form on our admissions page. Tours are offered Monday through Friday at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM, and select Saturdays at 11:00 AM.

What is the application deadline for the upcoming semester?

Application deadlines vary by program. For undergraduate programs, the early decision deadline is November 1, and the regular decision deadline is January 15 for fall admission. For graduate programs, please check the specific program's page or contact the Admissions Office.

How can I apply for financial aid or scholarships?

To apply for financial aid, you need to complete the FAFSA (Free Application for Federal Student Aid) form. For scholarships, complete our institutional scholarship application available on the Financial Aid page. Both are typically due by March 1 for the following academic year.

Where can I find information about housing options?

Information about on-campus residence halls and off-campus housing options can be found on our Housing page. For specific questions, contact the Housing Office at housing@university.edu or (123) 456-7895.

How can I request a transcript?

Current students can request transcripts through the Student Portal. Alumni and former students can submit a transcript request form available on the Registrar's page or contact the Registrar's Office at registrar@university.edu.
